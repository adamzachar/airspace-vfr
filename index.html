VFR Airspace Watch

A situational-awareness aid for VFR pilots on Apple Watch and iPhone: airspace alerts, altimeter hints, nearest airports, METAR, TAF and airport information for Europe.

Important: This app is an orientation aid only. It is not certified aviation equipment and not a navigation instrument. The official sources are the AIP, NOTAM and the flight information service. Aeronautical data is community-maintained (Open Flightmaps) and may be incomplete or outdated. Always check before flight.

Report wrong aeronautical data / Nahlásit chybu v datech

Airspace and airport data comes from Open Flightmaps. Errors in the data should be reported there so they are fixed for everyone. Weather comes from the NOAA Aviation Weather Center (aviationweather.gov).

FAQ

Does the app need an internet connection? The watch works offline with the built-in data for Czech Republic, Austria, Germany, Poland and Slovakia. Weather (METAR, TAF) and data updates need a connection.

Does the Apple Watch need the iPhone? No for flying with the built-in data. The iPhone is used to download more countries, update data (AIRAC cycle) and send the area around you to the watch.

Is my location sent anywhere? Your position stays on your device. To show weather the app asks NOAA for reports in an area around you. See the Privacy Policy.
